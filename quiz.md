# Data Visualization and Storytelling Quiz

## Question 1 (Easy)
According to the presentation, what are the two main purposes of data visualization?

A) Analysis and presentation
B) Exploration and explanation
C) Description and interpretation
D) Collection and storage

**Correct Answer: B) Exploration and explanation**

---

## Question 2 (Easy)
What does "Gestalt" mean?

A) Order
B) Pattern
C) Shape
D) Design

**Correct Answer: C) Shape**

---

## Question 3 (Medium)
According to Gestalt psychology, humans have limited memory and attention spans. Which two types of processing influence how we interpret visual information?

A) Sequential and parallel processing
B) Top-down and bottom-up processing
C) Conscious and unconscious processing
D) Logical and emotional processing

**Correct Answer: B) Top-down and bottom-up processing**

---

## Question 4 (Medium)
When should you use a text chart instead of a traditional graph or table?

A) When presenting multiple data points
B) When comparing different categories
C) When it's about a single number
D) When showing trends over time

**Correct Answer: C) When it's about a single number**

---

## Question 5 (Medium-Hard)
Which Gestalt principle is demonstrated when data points are arranged in ascending order (like the mtcars example sorted by mpg) to help viewers follow a logical progression?

A) Law of closure
B) Law of continuity
C) Law of similarity
D) Law of proximity

**Correct Answer: B) Law of continuity**

---

## Question 6 (Hard)
According to the presentation's guidance on reducing clutter, what role do empty spaces play in visualizations?

A) They should be minimized to fit more information
B) They are wasted space that reduces efficiency
C) They are as important as pauses in speaking
D) They should only be used for aesthetic purposes

**Correct Answer: C) They are as important as pauses in speaking**

---

## Question 7 (Hard)
The presentation discusses three key concepts from Gestalt psychology: reification, emergence, and Prägnanz. What does Prägnanz specifically refer to in the context of visual perception?

A) The ability to see hidden patterns in data
B) The tendency to perceive objects as regular, simple, and orderly
C) The process of filling in missing visual information
D) The grouping of similar visual elements

**Correct Answer: B) The tendency to perceive objects as regular, simple, and orderly**

---

## Question 8 (Very Hard)
In the context of creating effective data visualizations, which combination of strategies best exemplifies the integration of cognitive limitations awareness with Gestalt principles to achieve optimal audience comprehension?

A) Using maximum color variety to distinguish all data categories while maintaining consistent font sizes throughout
B) Implementing strategic color limitation with purposeful alignment along implicit lines, while leveraging similarity and proximity laws to reduce cognitive load
C) Maximizing data density to provide complete information while using bold fonts to ensure readability
D) Applying uniform styling across all elements while using only grayscale colors to maintain professional appearance

**Correct Answer: B) Implementing strategic color limitation with purposeful alignment along implicit lines, while leveraging similarity and proximity laws to reduce cognitive load**
